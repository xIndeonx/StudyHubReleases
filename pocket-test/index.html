StudyHub on this phone

Seven checks that show whether StudyHub can run here from your OneDrive. What you pick stays on this phone: only the results file you save yourself goes anywhere.

1. Open it from the Home Screen

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Then open StudyHub test from the Home Screen and do the checks there, not in Safari.
